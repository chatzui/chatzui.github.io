DATA & SECURITY

Your knowledge serves only you.

Not a promise: this is how the system is built.

  • "Every customer's knowledge is walled off"

    Every row is tied to your account; vector search runs in its own namespace. No one can read, write or delete anyone else's knowledge.

  • "Tokens are stored only as hashes"

    Your MCP token is emailed once; the system keeps only its SHA-256. Leaked? Reissue it — the old one stops working at once.

  • "Chat history stays on the visitor's device"

    Visitor conversations live in their own browser and clear after 30 days. The server keeps no visitor chats.

  • "The expertise stays on the server"

    Department prompts, processes and roles are assembled on the server only — never sent to the browser.

  • "You approve before it's final"

    The Secretary proposes, you approve the routing; each department pauses for approval at the key steps. Auto runs only when you turn it on.

  • "Plan ends, access ends — no lock-in"

    Cancel any time through PayPal. Knowledge loaded from your API stays yours — the source is still in your own systems.

STRAIGHT QUESTIONS, HONEST ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before picking a plan.

  • What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

    Starter is the consultant (svc-aide) with everything it does: your own knowledge base, loading over API/MCP, embedding on your site. Pro is all of Starter plus the AI Secretary (svc-talk) and all 9 departments doing multi-step work for you.

  • Do I need to code to embed it?

    No. Paste two lines (the script tag and the svc-aide tag with your owner, both in your email) into your site's custom HTML. The Guide walks through WordPress, Shopify and plain HTML.

  • Where does the knowledge come from?

    Any JSON API (products, FAQ, policies…) — fields are guessed and previewed; or let an AI like Claude or Cursor load it over MCP, even from a file or text you hand it. Reloading a source updates it, never duplicates.

  • What is MCP, do I need it?

    MCP is the standard that lets AIs (Claude, Cursor, VS Code…) use outside tools. With it, just say "load the new price list into the assistant" and the AI does it. Optional — loading from an API in Settings works too. The Guide has one-click install.

  • Where do the Secretary and departments run?

    In your canvas workspace (/system/ ), signed in with the account that bought the plan. E-commerce is open today; Education, F&B, Healthcare and Real Estate are under expert review before release.

  • Do I lose anything upgrading from Starter to Pro?

    No. Your knowledge base, MCP token and embed code stay the same; the Secretary and departments open right after the upgrade, using that same knowledge.

  • What happens if my plan lapses or I cancel?

    There are 3 grace days past the billing period. After that the embedded consultant and MCP stop serving your knowledge until you renew.
